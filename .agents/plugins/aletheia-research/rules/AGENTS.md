# The Aletheia Method — Default Research Protocol

Apply **The Aletheia Method** (*Portal-Driven Resonance & Adversarial Tomography*) by default whenever conducting scientific, mathematical, physical, biological, computational, engineering, or cross-repository research (single-agent or multi-agent swarm). For full multi-agent role templates, oracle matrices, CLI checks, and domain packs, load the `aletheia-research` skill.

## Core Discipline (The 5 Laws)

1. **Active Portal-Keeper Loop (Never Park Cartography):**
   Treat raw transcripts, lab notes, datasets, open issues, and rough drafts as live **portals into new frontiers**, not static documents to grade once and shelve. Whenever an experiment or probe hits an anomaly, dimensionless coincidence, counterexample, or sharpness wall, re-enter the raw source portals with that new lens before moving on.
2. **Steelman Before Falsifying:**
   Be radically open-minded to new truths before dismissing any idea as incorrect, infeasible, or "symbolic theater." Strictly separate *prototype friction* (indexing bugs, missing guards, rough stubs, over-strong lemma premises) from the underlying scientific or mathematical structure. Diagnose defects honestly, then formulate and test the **strongest repaired version** before closing any line of inquiry.
3. **Adversarial Verification as Active Multi-Basis Tomography ($\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{obs}}$):**
   Pair radical openness in hypothesis generation with uncompromising independent verification—never lower the rigor bar. Verify algebraic/combinatorial claims with zero-shared-code exact oracles (`int`, `fractions.Fraction`, `math.isqrt`, exact `SymPy`, or kernel-checked Lean/Coq) and empirical claims with out-of-sample seeds, held-out anchors, and matched adversarial nulls. Enforce R1–R5 gate contracts and R4 execution parity (`-I -B`, `-I -O -B`, `-I -B -W error`, zero bare `assert`s on trust paths). When an audit catches a defect, report the minimal counterexample *and* the exact structural tightening required if the core truth survives.
4. **Honest Novelty & Scope Accounting (`N0`/`N1`/`N2`):**
   Default every finding to `N0` (known / repackaging) until checked against actual full-text prior art and peer PRs (never titles from memory). Classify every claim's oracle class (`exact` / `sampled` / `anchor`) and state explicit scope limits (local vs. global, `uncompiled_source` if a proof assistant is absent, `Draft PR` until merged). Log every intermediate `FALSIFIED` or `PARTIAL/FRAGILE` audit permanently in `verification/AUDIT_LOG.md`.
5. **Exact-Operator Cross-Pollination (Zero Language Smuggling):**
   Never claim cross-domain unification from shared vocabulary alone ("sheaf", "curvature", "duality", "emergence"). Transfer discoveries across domains strictly via explicit typed objects, executable maps/identities, quantitative closure defects, or provable obstructions—and elevate proven obstructions into theorems or branch triggers rather than dead ends.
