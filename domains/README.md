## 2. Cross-Domain Operator Transfer Checklist (Law 5 Anti-Smuggling Protocol)

Cross-domain claims are where language smuggling is easiest. **Worked negative example:** the Cave Expedition's eight-way "exact Schur-complement unification" failed this checklist. An outside Gate-5 audit found Gate 5 not met for the eight-way claim (cave-expedition `verification/outside/gate5-unification/README.md:152-161`), and `AUDIT-085R` accepted its finding that calling the rows instances of one Schur-complement invariant has zero discriminating power (cave-expedition `symbiosis/round10/arm2-unification-and-novelty/REPORT_R10_2_UNIFICATION_AND_NOVELTY.md:104-106`). The surviving reading, in the outside audit's proposed wording: eight frontiers each use block elimination; one pair, `AUDIT-017` and `AUDIT-019`, is linked by an exact congruence; a shared invariant beyond the elimination identity has not been shown. The eight rows spanned `spectral-inertia` ($R_{\mathrm{opt}}$, Stieltjes–Hankel $R_{H4}$), `incentre-lattices` (sheaf elastic Schur & Loewner $G(s) \succeq G(1)$), `quotient-lp-bounds` (tropical Farkas / Doob peeling), `gravity` (AeST Hamiltonian Schur $S_P$), `witness-map` (Lindblad Zeno Schur), and Kigami fractal resistance. Earlier wording of this paragraph, kept as history: "Aletheia's highest-leverage breakthroughs occur *between* domain packs—such as the Cave Expedition's **8-Way Exact Schur-Complement Unification** spanning …".

Before claiming ANY bridge between Domain A and Domain B, pass all five checks:

1. **Typed Source & Target Objects:** Write down the exact mathematical objects on both sides (matrices, transition graphs, cellular sheaves, Lindblad superoperators, rational maps)—never prose nouns alone.
2. **Executable Map or Identity:** Implement a runnable, deterministic script verifying the exact operator relation (e.g., $S_P \equiv -(G_{AA}/\widetilde{G})/(4k^2)$, $K_q' \equiv D_h^{-1} K_q D_h$, $\sigma^* \equiv C^\top \operatorname{diag}(\tau^*) C + 2 \operatorname{diag}(\tau^*) C$) or quantifying the exact closure defect.
3. **Semiring / Regime Separation Test:** Actively try to *break* the analogy! If Domain A lives over $(+,\times)$ and Domain B over $(\min,+)$, or one is Euclidean and the other curved Bregman/Fisher–Rao, build explicit bidirectional counterexamples (`AUDIT-004`, `AUDIT-008R`) to locate the exact boundary where the bridge holds vs. fails.
4. **Independent Clean-Room Audit:** Submit the cross-domain instrument with explicit falsification criteria and must-fail controls to an independent verifier (`aletheia audit-check` + zero-shared-code check).
5. **Discriminating Power (Null Panel):** Run the bridge criterion on a seeded panel of matched unrelated systems, with scrambled-partition versions, and require their pass rate to stay below a margin declared before the run. A criterion that every applicable system passes, such as an exact textbook identity, is not evidence of a bridge. Worked example: cave-expedition's Gate-5 predicate test (`verification/outside/gate5-unification/predicate/null_panel.py`, `p_check.py`, `RESULTS.md`) ran the Schur-complement criteria read from the ledger on two seeded panels of 360 unrelated systems each. Every Hermitian or quadratic predicate passed in 100 percent of the systems where its hypothesis holds (`RESULTS.md:3`, `:9`). For every theorem predicate, native and scrambled partitions passed at the same rate within sampling noise (`RESULTS.md:39`); the audit's README adds that two tests that are not pure theorems, the integer sign rule (P3) and a mixing congruence (P4c), vary by a few systems under scrambling (`verification/outside/gate5-unification/README.md:30`). None of checks 1–4 measures this rate.
