# The Aletheia Method — Universal Agent Protocol

Apply **The Aletheia Method** (*Portal-Driven Resonance & Adversarial Tomography*) whenever conducting scientific, mathematical, physical, biological, computational, engineering, or cross-repository research (single-agent or multi-agent swarm).

## The 5 Operational Laws

1. **Active Portal-Keeper Loop (Never Park Cartography):**
   Treat raw transcripts, lab notebooks, datasets, open issues, and rough drafts as live **portals into new frontiers**, never static documents to grade once and shelve. Whenever a probe encounters an anomaly, dimensionless coincidence, counterexample, or sharpness wall, re-enter raw portals with that lens before moving on.
2. **Steelman Before Falsifying:**
   Be radically open-minded to new truths before dismissing any lead as incorrect, infeasible, or "symbolic theater." Separate *prototype friction* (indexing bugs, missing guards, rough stubs, over-strong lemma premises) from the underlying scientific or mathematical structure. State defects honestly, then build and test the **strongest repaired version** before closing any line of inquiry.
3. **Adversarial Verification as Active Multi-Basis Tomography ($\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{obs}}$):**
   Pair radical openness with uncompromising independent verification. Check algebraic/formal claims with zero-shared-code exact oracles (`int`, `Fraction`, `isqrt`, exact `SymPy`, Lean/Coq) and empirical claims with out-of-sample seeds, held-out anchors, and matched adversarial nulls. Enforce R1–R5 gate integrity and R4 parity (`python3 -I -B`, `-I -O -B`, `-I -B -W error`; zero bare `assert`s on trust paths). Report both the minimal counterexample *and* the exact structural repair if the core truth holds.
4. **Honest Novelty & Scope Accounting (`N0`/`N1`/`N2`):**
   Default findings to `N0` (repackaging) until checked against full-text prior art and live peer PRs (never titles from memory). Tag every claim's oracle class (`exact`/`sampled`/`anchor`) and scope limits (`uncompiled_source`, `Draft PR`, local vs. global). Log every `FALSIFIED` or `PARTIAL/FRAGILE` audit permanently in `verification/AUDIT_LOG.md`.
5. **Exact-Operator Cross-Pollination (Zero Language Smuggling):**
   Never claim cross-domain unification from shared vocabulary alone ("sheaf", "curvature", "duality", "emergence"). Transfer discoveries strictly via explicit typed objects, executable maps/identities, quantitative closure defects, or provable obstructions—and elevate proven obstructions into theorems or branch triggers rather than dead ends.

## Mandatory Execution & Hygiene Gates

- **Beyond-Language, Beyond-Numerology Perception (v1.2):** Strip disciplinary labels and base-10/coordinate numerology (*Stage A*), then compress relational silhouettes into coordinate-, unit-, and radix-free exact invariants or zero-residual null-equivalences (*Stage B*, `THEORY.md` §3.5) verified under independent basis/scale invariance tests.
- **Single-Agent Cycle:** Strictly separate four phases: **Portal** (`frontier_portals.md`) $\to$ **Probe** (executable against real upstream equations/data, zero proxy substitution) $\to$ **Tomography** (clean-room independent audit + `AUDIT_LOG.md`) $\to$ **Resonance** (re-enter portals with every surprise or obstruction).
- **CLI Verification (v1.2):** Run `python3 -m aletheia audit-check .` (`--lean-audit`, `--allow-project-import`, `--comparator-config`, `--pinned-manifest`, `--prereg-check`) and `python3 -m aletheia diagnose .` (`CS-01`–`CS-11`) before publishing; run `python3 -m aletheia mutate <script.py> --test-cmd "<cmd>"` (`--add-cwd`, dict/accumulator guards) for R5; use `aletheia ratchet` & `aletheia mde` for 5-gate loops and Plane-1 manifests.
- **Publishing Discipline:** Never merge to upstream default branches without explicit approval. Open clean **Draft PRs** with zero CRLF, zero personal paths, zero secrets, and zero `__pycache__` artifacts.
